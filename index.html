fbacc.io Bookmarklet

Drag this big button to your bookmarks bar, then click it on Facebook to inject the helper script.

★ fbacc
1
Show your bookmarks bar. In most browsers: Ctrl/Cmd + Shift + B.
2
Drag the button into the bar. Drop it between your other bookmarks to save it.
3
Open Facebook and click it. The script runs on the current page only when you click the bookmark.
Version 7 Bookmarklet runs only when clicked on a page.
